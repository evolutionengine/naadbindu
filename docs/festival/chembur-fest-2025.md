## Artists

### Pt Nirmalya Dey — Dhrupad Vocal

Pandit Nirmalya Dey is a leading Dhrupad vocalist known for his deep immersion in the Dagar tradition and his powerful, meditative renditions of ragas.

### Shri Prasanna Vishwanathan — Vocal

Shri Prasanna Vishwanathan is a dedicated practitioner of Dhrupad vocal music, bringing sincerity and depth to his performances.

### Shri Wonseak Chang — Surbahar

Shri Wonseak Chang is an accomplished exponent of the Surbahar, a bass variant of the sitar traditionally used for Dhrupad-style instrumental music. His playing captures the slow, contemplative essence of alap and jod.

### Pt Sanjay Agle & Shri Vivek Kurangale — Pakhawaj

Pandit Sanjay Agle and Shri Vivek Kurangale provided the rhythmic foundation on the Pakhawaj, the traditional percussion instrument of Dhrupad.

## About the Festival

The Chembur Dhrupad Festival is an annual event organized by the Indian School of Dhrupad to honour the memory of **Ustad Zia Mohiuddin Dagar** and **Ustad Zia Fariduddin Dagar**, two legendary figures who dedicated their lives to the propagation of Dhrupad music.

This is a completely community-driven initiative. If you wish to support this effort, please consider a donation of any amount.
